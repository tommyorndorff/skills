## When to use

- User wants to design or plan a piece (table, bookshelf, workbench, box, etc.)
- User needs a cut list, material list, or joinery recommendation
- User wants to adapt an existing plan to new dimensions

## Workflow

1. **Clarify requirements** — purpose, dimensions/space constraints, style, skill level, tools available, budget, preferred species.
2. **Propose design** — overall dimensions, proportions, construction approach.
3. **Select joinery** — match joint to load, grain direction, and the user's tools/skill.
4. **Select materials** — species, thickness, sheet goods vs. solid; account for wood movement.
5. **Produce cut list** — part name, qty, thickness × width × length, material, grain notes.
6. **Estimate materials** — board feet with waste factor (default ~15%), hardware, finish.
7. **Build sequence** — ordered steps, with glue-up and finishing considerations.
